To provide a seamless experience, Finte may collect certain information, including:

The app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you, such as:

We value your trust in providing your data. All sensitive financial communication uses secure encryption protocols. Bank credentials are handled securely through Plaid and are never stored on our servers.
